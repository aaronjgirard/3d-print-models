## Citation and published files
The dots are the stations of the 2023 Kilauea summit nodal array. Please cite:
- O'Connell, D. R. H., and A. J. Girard (2026). 2023 Kilauea horizontal-component signal/noise assessment of a large three-component nodal array. *Seismica*, 5(2). https://doi.org/10.26443/seismica.v5i2.3013
- Kramer, R. L., R. Denlinger, A. J. Girard, D. R. H. O'Connell, S. Roecker, G. Lin, N. Bennington, A. Flinders, and E. Vinarski (submitted). A high-density, active and passive seismic imaging experiment at the summit of Kilauea Volcano, Hawai'i. *Seismological Research Letters*.
- Denlinger, R., G. Lin, M. Haney, S. Roecker, N. Bennington, A. Flinders, D. O'Connell, and E. Vinarski (2023). Active and passive seismic interferometry of Kilauea volcano [Data set]. International Federation of Digital Seismograph Networks. https://doi.org/10.7914/gqdb-hs97

The public repo carries only the current print, `kilauea_190mm_4x_dots0p9_scale.stl`, and the dot width test. The older STLs and the source 3MF named below are git-ignored. Model and scripts are Apache-2.0.

- Source mesh: `source/kilauea_190mm_4x_dots_v2.stl`, local only and git-ignored like the rest of `source/` (same 1,375,308 triangles as plate 1 of `kilauea_relief_receiver_holes_190mm_NEW.3mf`). 190 x 190 mm, 46.88 mm tall, 1830 receiver dots 0.7 mm across. The west face carries "Kilauea May-June 2023" recessed 0.8 mm.
- Horizontal scale, recovered 2026-11-11 by fitting the dots to the station UTM coordinates in `c14_station_meta.txt` from the survey processing files, not in this repo (UTM zone 5): model origin at E 254000, N 2138500; 190 mm = 15.50 km, so 1 mm = 81.579 m, scale 1:81,579, no rotation. 1803 of 1830 dots fall within 10 m of a station (median 0.1 m). No build script for the model was found.
- `kilauea_190mm_4x_dots_v2_scale.stl`: adds "1:81,579" recessed 0.8 mm on the west face, Arial Bold, 7 mm cap height, z 17 to 24 mm, centered over the word "Kilauea" (made with `../WA_DNR/add_wall_text.py ... --side west --zc 20.5 --along 30.65`).
- `kilauea_190mm_4x_dots_v2_scale_PLAplus_0.20mm_12h22m.gcode`: that model sliced with `../cli_slice` (style `relief`) (same settings as the WA DNR print). 234 layers, 46.85 mm, 262.2 g. Expect about 13.5 h (the logo test ran 10% over its estimate). Re-sliced 2026-11-12 06:37 MST with layer markers for Mainsail and `3dstatus`; the print moves are identical to the first slice.
- `kilauea_relief_receiver_dots_190mm_NEW_PLA_0.2_1d4h38m.gcode` was sliced on the Marlin `Sovol SV06 0.4 - park` preset; do not print it.
- `kilauea_190mm_4x_dots0p9.stl`: the source mesh (no scale text) with the dots widened from 0.7 to 0.9 mm (chosen 2026-11-13 from `tests/dot_width_sweep`, where 0.7 mm dots snapped off). Made with `python widen_dots.py source/kilauea_190mm_4x_dots_v2.stl kilauea_190mm_4x_dots0p9.stl --dot_d 0.9`: each old 16-sided dot is found by its top cap and a 32-sided 0.9 mm dot unioned on the same centre, 1.2 mm above the highest terrain under it (never below the old top) and 0.6 mm below the lowest. 1807 dots; the other 23 of the 1830 stations were already hidden under taller neighbours. 17 dots mostly covered by neighbours are centred on their exposed piece (at most about 0.2 mm off). On the scale-text model every old dot lay inside its new one (old minus new: 0.00004 mm3) and nothing was added within 1.5 mm of a side wall. 11% of the dots touch a neighbour.
- `kilauea_190mm_4x_dots0p9_scale.stl`: that model with two centred lines recessed 0.8 mm into the north wall, Arial Bold, 7 mm cap height: "1:81,579" at z 14.5 to 21.5 mm over "VE 4×" at z 4 to 11 mm (`../WA_DNR/add_wall_text.py ... --side north --zc 18`, then `--zc 7.5`). The north wall top is 31 to 37 mm along the text. The west wall keeps only "Kilauea May-June 2023".
- `kilauea_190mm_4x_dots0p9_scale_PLAplus_0.20mm_11h07m.gcode`: that model sliced with `../cli_slice` (style `relief`). Same settings as the 12h22m slice except sparse infill adaptive cubic (the relief preset since 2026-11-12 20:42). 234 layers, 46.85 mm, 235.3 g, Klipper estimate 11h07m with margin.
